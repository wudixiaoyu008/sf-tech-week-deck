Like a hiring manager, I start with the job description.

What does success look like?

Knowledge

What context do you load?

Tools

What can you do?

Autonomy

What do you do alone, check, ask about, or hand off?

Response

How do you show up: text, a component, a plan, a next step?

The core design decision

The autonomy line

Do it

Look up, research, read.

Check

Ambiguous? Clarify. Several valid paths? Let them steer.

Ask

Money, sending, anything irreversible.

Hand off

Repeated failure, or they want a person.

After OpenAI, A Practical Guide to Building Agents

The job description, written down

It’s a system prompt.

1# Acme Audio · Support Agent 2You help customers resolve order and product issues in one conversation. 3## Decide on your own 4Replacements or repairs under $100, when the item is in warranty. 5## Ask the customer first 6Any refund. Show the exact amount and wait for confirmation. 7## Hand off to a human 8Refunds over $200. A second failed attempt. Any time they ask for a person. 9## How to respond 10Lead with the answer. And where I want to grow · 5 of 5

Building isn’t the hard part anymore.

I can ship a product in a weekend. Getting people to use it is what I’m learning now: distribution, storytelling, business.
