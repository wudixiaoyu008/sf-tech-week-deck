Part 2 · Its job description

Designing the agent’s behavior

Like a hiring manager, I start with the job description.

Role

Who are you?

Goal

What does success look like?

Knowledge

What context do you load?

Tools

What can you do?

Autonomy

What do you do alone, check, ask about, or hand off?

Response

How do you show up: text, component, data viz, actions?

The job description, written down

It’s a system prompt.

1# Juniper Audio · Support Agent 2You help customers fix order and product issues. 3## Knowledge 4Orders, open tickets, return and warranty policy. 5## Tools 6look_up_order · create_return · issue_refund 7## Decide on your own 8Repairs or replacements under $100, in warranty. 9## Ask the customer first 10Any refund. Show the amount, wait for a yes. 11## Hand off to a human 12Over $200, a second failed try, or they ask. 13## How to respond 14Lead with the answer. Use {cards} and {buttons}.

Iterating toward the balance

Enough

Context and instructions to do the job.

Not too much

Or it fills the context window.

Not too strict

Or it limits what the model can do.

Illustrative example.

The model picks what to show.

My headphones stopped working.
RepairReplaceRefund $89

Follow up as a message.

Your orders shown as cards.

Solutions to repair, replace, or refund.

One request, many responses.
